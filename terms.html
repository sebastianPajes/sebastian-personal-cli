Use of this integration

Updated September 26, 2026.

This OAuth integration is maintained for its owner's personal use. It is not a public hosted service and does not offer subscriptions, purchases, service-level commitments, or access to the owner's accounts.

Account access requires the account owner's explicit Google authorization. Commands are used only for accounts and actions the owner is authorized to access and perform. Google services and the underlying gog software remain subject to their respective terms and licenses.

The owner controls the local configuration and can stop using the integration, revoke Google access, and remove local credentials at any time. The privacy policy explains data handling.
