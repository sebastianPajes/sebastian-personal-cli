Personal Google account tools

Sebastian Personal CLI

A local command-line integration for the owner's Google account.

This personal OAuth application connects the gog command-line tool to Google APIs. Its permissions support searching and reading Gmail, sending email when explicitly requested by the owner, and read-only access to Google Drive files, Google Docs documents, and Google Calendar events.

The command-line tool runs on the owner's computer. This website provides application information only; it has no sign-in form and does not receive Gmail messages, OAuth tokens, or other Google account data.

Access is controlled by the owner

Google access is granted through Google's consent screen and can be revoked through Google Account connections. This integration is maintained for the owner's personal use and is not offered as a public hosted email service.
